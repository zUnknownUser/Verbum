# Temas / Themes

The discovery module offers eight editorial categories, three starting points
(Faith, Prayer, Hope), localized search with aliases, A–Z filters and pages of 30.
Category selection resets search, letter and pagination. Returning from a detail
preserves the selection. Empty, loading and retry states use the existing catalog
flow on both iOS and Android.

The bilingual catalog contains **80 themes**, **240 ordered reading selections**
and **57 links between themes with overlapping passages**. The ten existing theme
IDs are retained; 70 new stable IDs use `editorial.theme.*`. Each theme has English
and Portuguese names, summaries and editorial classification. Synonyms are supplied
in each language where useful; searches do not mix localized aliases.

Categories: Life with God; Emotions and difficulties; Relationships; Character and
choices; Everyday life; Foundations of faith; Life in community; Life and eternity.
Some themes appear in multiple categories without duplicating their detail page.
This is a curated starting catalog, not an exhaustive taxonomy of the Bible.

Theme detail pages place the three reading selections immediately after the
introduction. Reading opens the existing reader and its selected translation.
Related themes, graph navigation and sources remain available. The classifications
and summaries are editorial navigation aids, not quotations or a claim of consensus
among Christian traditions. Baptism, the Lord's Supper and future events are worded
as invitations to read their passages rather than imposing a denominational position.

## Content workflow

- Authoring: `pipeline/sources/themes/catalog-{pt,en}.tsv`.
- Builder: `python -m verbum_pipeline.themes --chapters <canonical-text-directory> --output sources/themes/catalog.json`.
- The builder checks all selected verse ranges against the chapter texts, preserves
  the ten old IDs, and emits source references and bilingual presentation metadata.
- Version 4 editorial bundles add `themes` metadata; earlier bundle serialization
  and review hashes remain unchanged. Review includes the discovery metadata.
- Publish through the existing reviewed, atomic and idempotent publication path.
  A changed catalog needs a new hash-bound review. No paid model is called.
- Apply `0009_theme_discovery.sql` before deploying the category-filtering API.

API: `GET /v1/entities?type=theme&lang=pt&category=emotions&q=preocupacao&limit=30`.
English equivalent: `lang=en&q=worry`. A category on a non-theme request is rejected.

## Audio decision

The owner declined paid pre-generation and requested continuing with Themes.
No synthesis was triggered. A read-only inventory found 5 reusable synchronized
Portuguese database artifacts out of 1,189 canonical chapters for the active
narrator. Disk-only remote artifacts were not audited. The optional, explicit
batch-generation tool is documented in `backend/AUDIO_LIBRARY.md`; nothing is
scheduled or enabled automatically.
